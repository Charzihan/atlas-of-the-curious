EXPERIMENT 001

A planet.
An alphabet.

Earthxt asks a simple question: what if the world was made of text?

Every mark on this globe is a character, positioned with the mathematics of a 3D sphere. As you approach the surface, coarse land gives way to coastlines, country borders, and names.

The globe runs entirely in your browser, with a small local dataset and no mapping services. Synthetic mode lets you explore the same engine with invented geography.
