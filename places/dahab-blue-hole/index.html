The Blue Hole, Dahab

البلو هول، دهب

Egypt — Gulf of Aqaba, Sinai Peninsula

A 100-metre-deep hole in the reef that glows sapphire from above.

A natural sinkhole in the coral reef, 30 metres across and over 100 metres deep. From the surface it looks like a perfect blue eye; from underwater, a tunnel runs at 55 metres and opens into a vast cavern. It is one of the world’s most famous — and most feared — dive sites: the current and the depth make it lethal for the unprepared.

Field note

The Blue Hole was the site of the 1960s 'Blue Hole' expedition led by Jacques Cousteau, which established its depth and mapped its interior.
