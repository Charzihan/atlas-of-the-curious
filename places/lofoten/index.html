Lofoten Islands

Lofoten

Norway — Lofoten, Nordland County, northern Norway

A jagged archipelago where the sea, the peaks, and the aurora share the same frame.

The Lofoten archipelago is a chain of low, steep islands that climb out of the warm Gulf Stream waters near 68° north — far enough north for the midnight sun and the northern lights, but mild enough to hold reindeer and fishing villages. The 'fjeld' peaks rise almost vertically from the water, and the traditional red fishing cabins, or rorbu, sit on little piers over the deep sea. In winter the aurora borealis lights up the same sky as the peaks.

Field note

The name Lofoten literally means 'the place of the herring'; the warm North Atlantic Current keeps the waters ice-free, which is why the islands are far milder than other places at the same latitude.
