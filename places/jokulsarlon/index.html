Glaciers & Ice

Jökulsárlón

Iceland — Southeast Iceland, below Vatnajökull

A lagoon of drifting blue icebergs under a sky that never quite darkens in summer.

Where the Vatnajökull glacier calves into the sea, Jökulsárlón collects icebergs that drift from deep blue to pure white before washing up on the nearby 'Diamond Beach', where they lie on black sand like jewels. The lagoon is so shallow in places that you can see the ice from above and below at once.

Field note

The lagoon was created in 1934 when a glacier dammed a river; it has since grown to over 18 square kilometres and is still expanding.
