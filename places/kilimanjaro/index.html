Mount Kilimanjaro

Mlima Kilimanjaro

Tanzania — Kilimanjaro Region, northern Tanzania

A free-standing volcano that climbs from savanna to a permanent ice cap.

Kilimanjaro, at 5,895 metres, is the highest mountain in Africa and one of the largest free-standing volcanoes on Earth. Because it rises so abruptly from the surrounding plains, a single climb passes through equatorial savanna, cloud forest, alpine desert, and — at the summit — a permanent ice cap. The peak, Uhuru Peak, is high enough that its ice is being lost faster than it is replaced, and the glaciers have already shrunk to a fraction of their early-1900s extent.

Field note

Kilimanjaro is a dormant stratovolcano; its glaciers are a UNESCO World Heritage Site and are projected to disappear within a few decades at current rates of loss.
