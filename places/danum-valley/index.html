Forests & Jungles

Danum Valley

Lembah Danum

Malaysia (Borneo) — Sabah, Malaysian Borneo

One of the last untouched blocks of lowland rainforest on Earth.

4,500 hectares of continuous lowland rainforest that has barely been logged, ringed by mountain jungle. It holds pygmy elephants, orangutans, clouded leopards, and over 400 species of bird. The canopy walkway lets you walk 20 metres up in the treetops, where the forest sounds completely different: quieter, hotter, and full of life you can’t hear from the ground.

Field note

The Danum Valley Conservation Area was set aside in 1992 and has never been logged; it is one of the best-studied rainforest sites in Asia.

Best time to visitYear-round (equatorial), though May–Oct is a touch drier
Nearest major citySandakan, Sabah (≈ 1 hour by boat)
