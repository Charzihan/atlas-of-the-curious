Perito Moreno Glacier

Glaciar Perito Moreno

Argentina — Los Glaciares National Park, Patagonia

The only large glacier on Earth that still advances — and crashes.

Most glaciers are retreating; Perito Moreno is one of the few that is roughly in balance, and every few years its lobe pushes out over Lago Argentino and dams the channel until the ice, 60 metres high above the waterline, collapses in a thunderous crash. The collapse can be seen from a viewing platform a few hundred metres away, and it sounds like the earth clearing its throat.

Field note

The glacier is about 30 kilometres long and 5 kilometres wide; the ice is up to 200 metres thick, and the blue you see is from the ice itself, not the sky.

Best time to visitDec–Mar (Patagonian summer) — calving is most dramatic then
