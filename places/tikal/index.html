Sacred & Ancient

Tikal

Guatemala — Petén Basin, northern Guatemala

A Maya city of 300+ structures, half of which are still buried in the jungle.

Tikal was one of the most powerful Maya cities for over a thousand years, at its peak home to perhaps 90,000 people. The six tallest pyramids still visible rise 70 metres above the jungle canopy, and the tallest, Temple IV, is the tallest structure built by the Maya. But the jungle has been closing over the site for centuries, and an estimated 300 structures are still buried under the trees, visible only from the air.

Field note

The Maya at Tikal played a form of ball game in a stadium that could hold thousands of spectators; the game may have had religious significance, and some players were sacrificed after important matches.
