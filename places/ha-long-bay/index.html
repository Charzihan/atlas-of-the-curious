Ha Long Bay

Vịnh Hạ Long

Vietnam — Quang Ninh Province, northern Vietnam

Nearly 2,000 limestone islands where the sea learned to float stone.

Legend says a mother dragon descended to fight a sea monster and spat out strings of jewels that became these islands. Geology agrees with the outline: karst towers of ancient limestone, eroded into spires, arches, and caves, drowned when sea levels rose. You can kayak through hangings caves that are hundreds of metres deep and climb to the top of a tower for a view over an ocean of stone.

Field note

Ha Long Bay has been a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1992. Around 50,000 people still live on floating villages in the bay.

Best time to visitOct–Dec and April, for calm seas and less haze
Nearest major cityHanoi (≈ 170 km)
