Deserts & Dunes

Erg Chebbi

عرق الشبي

Morocco — Drâa-Tafilalet Region, southeast Morocco

The Sahara’s most famous dune sea — and a desert that sings at night.

A chain of dunes stretching over 40 kilometres, with peaks up to 150 metres. The sand shifts colour with the sun — amber at dawn, rose at sunset, deep purple under the stars. On cold, still nights the dunes can 'sing': a low rumble produced when wind-blown sand grains, of a very specific size, cascade down the dune faces and vibrate against each other.

Field note

The 'singing dunes' phenomenon is studied by acousticians: only dunes with grains between roughly 0.1 and 0.5 mm in diameter produce the sound.

Best time to visitOct–May; nights are best to hear the 'singing dunes'
Nearest major cityRissani (≈ 30 km)
