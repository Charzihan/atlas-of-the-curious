Marble Caves (Grottoes of General Carrera)

Chile — Aysén Region, Lake General Carrera, Chile/Argentina border

A cathedral of banded stone beneath turquoise water.

On the Chilean shore of South America’s largest lake, freshwater waves have spent thousands of years dissolving and sculpting the marble into caverns. The water itself is the light source: turquoise light filters through the surface and plays across walls banded in green, blue, and white, making the whole cave look like a stained-glass window that slowly moves with the wind.

Field note

The caves’ colour shifts through the day with the sun’s angle; at dawn the walls glow pale blue, and by midday they turn deep emerald.

Best time to visitOct–Mar (Chilean summer), when light reaches deepest
Nearest major cityPuerto Natales (≈ 110 km)
