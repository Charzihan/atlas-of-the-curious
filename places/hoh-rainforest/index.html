Hoh Rainforest

United States — Olympic National Park, Washington State

The 'Mistlands' — a temperate rainforest that drinks the sky.

On the western coast of Washington, ocean fog rolls in from the Pacific and the forest literally drinks it. The Hoh rainforest is one of the largest temperate rainforests in the US: 3,000-year-old Sitka spruce, mossy arches, and a green so deep it has its own word, 'Mistlands'. It gets over 3 metres of precipitation a year, most of it as fog.

Field note

The Hoh River runs clear and cold even in winter because the forest’s dense canopy and cool soil keep it shaded; the water is one of the coldest freshwater systems on the US West Coast.
