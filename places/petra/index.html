Petra

البتراء

Jordan — Ma'an Governorate, southern Jordan

A whole city carved out of rose-red sandstone, hidden for over 1,000 years.

Petra was the capital of the Nabataean kingdom, a trading empire that controlled the incense routes between Arabia and the Mediterranean. The city is not built — it is carved, hundreds of tombs, temples, and public buildings cut into the face of sandstone cliffs. The most famous, the Treasury (Al-Khazneh), was carved from the top down, and for over a millennium after the city’s abandonment in the 6th century it was known only to local Bedouin, who kept its location secret from outsiders.

Field note

Petra’s water system, carved into the rock, could supply water to up to 30,000 people — a remarkable engineering feat for a city in the desert.
