Komodo National Park

Indonesia — Lesser Sunda Islands, between Bali and Sulawesi

A marine park that shares its beaches with the world’s largest living lizard.

Komodo Island is the only home of the komodo dragon — a 3-metre predator that evolved here in near-isolation. But the park is bigger than the dragon: 2,400 square kilometres of coral reefs, white-sand beaches, and clear water, making it one of the top dive destinations on Earth. On Padar Island you can stand on a volcanic peak and look down on two crescent beaches at once.

Field note

The komodo dragon’s bite delivers a battery of venomous glands in its lower jaw — confirmed by a 2009 study — not just bacteria in its mouth, as once believed.
