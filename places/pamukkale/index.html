Pamukkale

Türkiye — Denizli Province, southwestern Türkiye

A mountain built floor by floor by hot springs.

Mineral-rich spring water cascades down a hillside and hardens as it cools, laying down millimetre-thin sheets of travertine. Over millennia this built white terraces that look like frozen waterfalls — and the ancient city of Hierapolis, built on the rim around 190 BC, used the warm pools as a spa. Today you can walk the terraces and bathe in the ancient thermal pools beneath a marble theatre.

Field note

The terraces are a UNESCO World Heritage Site, and you walk them barefoot because shoes scuff the travertine that is still forming. The pool water holds around 35°C in winter.

Best time to visitApril–June and Sept–Oct, for warm water and mild light
Nearest major cityDenizli (≈ 18 km)
