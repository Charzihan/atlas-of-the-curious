Deserts & Dunes

Sossusvlei and Deadvlei

Namibia — Namib-Naukluft National Park, Namib Desert

The tallest dunes on Earth, beside a salt pan of ancient dead trees.

Sossusvlei is a basin at the foot of some of the tallest sand dunes in the world, several of them over 300 metres high and a vivid, iron-rich orange that deepens at sunset. Nearby, Deadvlei is a clay pan that was, about 900 years ago, ringed by a forest of camel-thorn trees; when the dunes blocked the river that fed them, the trees died but did not decay in the dry air, leaving a field of blackened, branchless trunks standing against a deep-orange dune backdrop.

Field note

Deadvlei's 'dead' trees have stood upright for hundreds of years because the dry, sealed environment stops them rotting; the dunes here among the highest on the planet.

Best time to visitApril–May and September–October, when it is warm enough to hike and the air is clear
