The Medina of Fez

فاس

Morocco — Fez, northwestern Morocco

A 900-year-old maze of 9,000 streets, with no cars, no roads, and no map that works.

The Fez el-Bali medina is the largest car-free urban area in the world, and the oldest continuously inhabited city in Morocco. It has been expanding organically for over 900 years, with streets narrowing and doubling back until they form a maze that no single person can fully navigate. The medina is home to the Al-Qarawiyyin mosque, founded in 859, which is the oldest continuously operating university in the world. Getting lost is not a failure here — it is the point.

Field note

The Al-Qarawiyyin mosque and madrasa, founded by Fatima al-Fihri in 859, is recognised by UNESCO as the oldest surviving institution of higher learning in the world.

Best time to visitMarch–May and September–October, when the desert heat and cold are both avoided
