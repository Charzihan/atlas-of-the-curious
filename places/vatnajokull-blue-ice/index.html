Vatnajökull Blue Ice Caves

Íshellar í Vatnajökli

Iceland — Vatnajökull National Park, southeast Iceland

Caves that exist for only a few months each year, inside the largest glacier in Europe.

Vatnajökull, the largest glacier in Europe by volume, forms natural caves every winter as meltwater carves tunnels into the ice. The ice is so dense and old that it scatters all colours of light except blue, which is why the caves glow a deep, almost impossible blue. Each year the caves shift, collapse, or form anew — the same cave rarely survives two seasons.

Field note

The ice in Vatnajökull is up to 9,000 years old at its core; the blue caves only form in the newer, more compressed surface layers.

Best time to visitMar–May, when the winter caves are safest
