Thimphu

ཐིམ་ཕུག

Bhutan — Thimphu Valley, central Bhutan

A capital city with no traffic lights — and a rule that says you must smile.

Thimphu, the capital of Bhutan, is one of the few cities in the world that has no traffic lights. Instead, a single traffic warden at the main junction directs cars, and the city’s compact size and gentle traffic make it workable. Bhutan also has a national dress code for men (the ghō) and women (the kira), and a 'Gross National Happiness' index that replaced GDP as the country’s primary measure of progress in the 1970s.

Field note

Thimphu is the only capital city in the world with no traffic lights; the city also bans plastic bags and charges for cigarette packs to discourage smoking.
