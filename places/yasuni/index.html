Yasuní

Parque Nacional Yasuní

Ecuador — Amazon Basin, eastern Ecuador

The most biodiverse hectare of forest on Earth.

A corner of the eastern Amazon so rich in life that ecologists have called it the most biodiverse place on the planet: over 6,000 species of butterflies, 600+ bird species, and a single hectare of forest that can hold more tree species than an entire European country. The river itself is a highway for pink river dolphins, caimans, and the giant otter.

Field note

Yasuní is also home to at least four isolated indigenous groups who have had no outside contact — their territory is legally protected so they can remain so.

Best time to visitDry season (Dec–May), though it is a true rainforest year-round
Nearest major cityPuyo or Quito (≈ 200–350 km)
Approximate coordinates0.500° S, 75.300° W
