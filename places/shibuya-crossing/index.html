Shibuya Scramble Crossing

渋谷スクランブル交差点

Japan — Tokyo, Shibuya Ward

The busiest pedestrian crossing on Earth — 3,000 people at once, eight directions at once.

Every 90 seconds the lights go green in all eight directions at once, and up to 3,000 people cross in a single wave. The crossing is a piece of urban choreography: no one looks at their phone, no one stops, and the flow is so smooth that it has become a landmark in its own right, appearing in countless films and documentaries. The giant screen above the crossing has been running since 1978.

Field note

The Shibuya Scramble was designed in 1962 and has been expanded several times; it now handles around 1,000 people per crossing cycle, and is one of the most-photographed urban spaces in the world.
