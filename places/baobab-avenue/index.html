Avenue of the Baobabs

Allée des Baobabs

Madagascar — Mongoly, western Madagascar

A row of 800-year-old bottle trees lining a dead riverbed.

A stretch of road lined with giant baobabs, some over 800 years old and 20 metres tall, their trunks up to 9 metres in diameter. They line the bed of a river that no longer flows, and the trees have been here long enough that the local Antandroy people wove them into their origin myths. At sunset the silhouettes look like giants standing watch over a vanished river.

Field note

Baobabs store up to 120,000 litres of water in their trunks — enough to survive years of drought, and enough to water a village.

Best time to visitMay–Oct (dry season), for the clearest light and sunsets
Nearest major cityMorondava (≈ 35 km)
Approximate coordinates23.140° S, 43.500° E
