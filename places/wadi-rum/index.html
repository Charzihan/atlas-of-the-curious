Wadi Rum

وادي رم

Jordan — Southern Jordan, near the Saudi border

The 'Valley of the Moon' that NASA used to rehearse for Mars.

A vast desert of rust-red sand, black basalt, and towering sandstone towers, carved by wind and flash floods over millions of years. Bedouin tribes have called it home for centuries, and their rock art — over 20,000 inscriptions and carvings — covers the valley walls, from prehistoric gazelles to medieval poetry.

Field note

Because its geology resembles the Martian surface, Wadi Rum has served as a training and rehearsal site for space agencies and sci-fi film crews, including parts of 'The Martian'.

Best time to visitOct–Apr, when the desert is cool enough to walk at night
Nearest major cityAqaba (≈ 60 km)
