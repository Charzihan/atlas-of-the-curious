Salar de Uyuni

Bolivia — Altiplano, southwest Bolivia

The world’s largest mirror, four kilometres above the sea.

When a prehistoric lake drained millions of years ago it left the largest salt flat on Earth: 10,582 square kilometres of white, so perfectly flat that you cannot see the horizon in any direction. In the rainy season a few centimetres of rain turn the surface into a flawless mirror of sky and stars — the famous 'Sky Mirror' — so you can walk as if floating in space.

Field note

Roughly half of the world’s lithium reserves are locked in the brine beneath this flat, making it both a wonder and a quiet geopolitical prize.

Best time to visitRainy season (Dec–Mar) for the 'Sky Mirror'; dry season (Jun–Aug) for the flat
Nearest major citySucre or La Paz (≈ 260–400 km)
