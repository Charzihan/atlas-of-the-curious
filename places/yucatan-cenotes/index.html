Cenotes of the Yucatán

Cenotes de Yucatán

Mexico — Yucatán Peninsula, Mexico

Thousands of open sinkholes where Maya ceremonies met the underworld.

The Yucatán is a giant underwater cavern system of dissolved limestone. Where the ceilings collapsed, open shafts of clear, cold water formed: cenotes. Some are shallow and sunlit, some are dark caves you swim through with a single beam of light. The Maya believed cenotes were doors to Xibalba, the underworld, and some still hold ritual offerings, including gold and human remains.

Field note

There are an estimated 30,000+ cenotes on the peninsula, and they are the only water source for much of the region — the surface rivers simply sink into the ground.

Best time to visitNov–Mar (dry season), when the water is clearest
