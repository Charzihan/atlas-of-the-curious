Baltoro Glacier

Pakistan — Gilgit-Baltistan, Karakoram range

The longest glacier outside the polar regions — 63 kilometres of moving ice.

Baltoro is the longest glacier in the world outside Antarctica and Greenland, stretching 63 kilometres from the Karakoram peaks down to the Indus river. It is also the starting point for most expeditions to K2, the second-highest mountain on Earth, which rises 6,000 metres above the glacier’s surface. The glacier itself is a landscape of seracs, crevasses, and blue ice that changes shape with every season.

Field note

Baltoro was first surveyed in 1892 by the Austrian explorer Eduard von Schwanbaur, who was one of the first non-local Europeans to walk its full length.
