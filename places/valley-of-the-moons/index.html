Valley of the Moon

Mondlandschaft

Namibia — Erongo Region, central Namibia

Rusting iron towers in a desert that eats its own mountains.

These 'moonstones' are boulders that rolled down the Brandberg massif, where iron in their cores oxidised into a rust-red crust. Over decades they 'melt' into their own bases as the wind and heat gnaw them, leaving a field of red monoliths that look weathered by time rather than wind. At dusk the whole valley glows like an ember field.

Field note

The nearby Brandberg shelters the 'Brandberg Paintings', rock art dating to roughly 6,000 years old — among the oldest in southern Africa.

Best time to visitMay–Oct, the cool dry season
